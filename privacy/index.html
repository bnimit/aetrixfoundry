Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 August 2026

Aetrix Foundry is an independent Mac software studio run by Nimit Bhandari. This policy explains what data the Duplex app and this website handle. The short version: Duplex collects no analytics or tracking, and the only data it ever sends anywhere is your license key when you activate it.

The Duplex app

Duplex runs entirely on your Mac. It contains no analytics, telemetry, tracking, or advertising. It does not create an account or profile of you.

The only time Duplex communicates over the network is license handling. When you activate a license, and periodically afterward to confirm the license is still valid, Duplex sends two things to the license provider (Dodo Payments):

  • your license key, and
  • the name of your Mac (used to identify the activation so you can move between machines).

Nothing else is transmitted. If you never enter a license, Duplex makes no network requests at all.

Instances you create

Duplex creates isolated copies of other apps (for example Claude or Slack) with their own local data folders on your Mac. Duplex does not read, collect, or transmit the contents of those instances. Each wrapped app is governed by its own developer's privacy policy.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Dodo Payments, which acts as the merchant of record. They collect the billing information needed to complete your purchase under their own privacy policy. Aetrix Foundry receives limited order information (such as your email address and license key) to deliver your license and provide support. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

This website

aetrixfoundry.com is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. Standard server logs may be kept by the host for security and reliability.

Your choices

You can deactivate a license from within Duplex at any time, which removes that activation from the license provider. To ask what order information we hold, or to request its deletion, email dev@aetrixfoundry.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date above will change and the current version will always live at this URL.

Contact
