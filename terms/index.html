Duplex

Duplex generates lightweight wrapper apps that let you run additional instances of Electron and Chromium based apps you already have installed, each with its own data folder. Duplex does not include or redistribute those third party apps, and never modifies your original installation. Your use of any wrapped app remains subject to that app's own terms.

License and pricing. Your first instance is free forever. A one time payment of 9 US dollars unlocks unlimited instances and grants a license key valid for activation on up to 2 Macs at a time. The license does not expire. You may move it between Macs by deactivating it on one and activating it on another.

Orlo
